Data Sources I Use

Financial & Banking Data

  • WRDS: CRSP, Compustat, IBES, TRACE, DealScan
  • FFIEC: U.S. bank call reports & regulatory filings
  • SEC EDGAR: corporate filings, full-text search
  • FRED: macro & financial time series
  • FDIC BankFind: bank financials & failures

Economics & Development Data

  • World Bank Open Data
  • IMF Data
  • CEPII: gravity & trade databases
  • GTAP: global trade analysis
  • U.S. Census Bureau
