Research Projects

International Growth Centre (IGC), Bangladesh

Bangladesh Green Industries Diagnostic

Analyzed Bangladeshi industries and helped build the Bangladesh Green Industries Diagnostic tool, which traces the root causes of weak progress on inclusive green growth.

USDA

Food Retailers and Their Composition in the USA

Analyzed U.S. food-retail establishments and used dollar-store establishment data to identify growth trends.

WorldFish

Baseline Survey of the Artemia for Bangladesh Project

Ran a baseline survey of 400 salt farmers in coastal Bangladesh. The report appeared in the WorldFish annual report.

Asian Development Bank (ADB)

Mobile Financial Services and Microfinance Institutions

Monitored and evaluated ADB-funded census and survey data on mobile financial services and microfinance, and contributed to questionnaire design and report writing.

Asian Development Bank (ADB), SEIP

Light Engineering and Electronics Sector Survey

Monitored surveys for the Skills for Employment Investment Program and evaluated the data for a labor-market study.

BIDS

COVID-19 and Customs Data

Prepared customs and NBR data sets for regression and trend analysis in a paper on the resilience of global value chains during the pandemic.

BIDS

COVID-19 and SMEs

Geospatial analysis of SME data, with maps built in R using ggplot2 and tmap.

Planning Commission, Bangladesh

Skills and Education for the 8th Five Year Plan

Analyzed education-sector data and helped write background papers on skills and education for the 8th Five Year Plan.
