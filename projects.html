BankPulse

Bank-health analytics that read the condition of the banking system before conventional metrics react.

BankPulse turns public regulatory filings into forward-looking measures of bank risk. The engine ingests quarterly call-report data across the U.S. banking system, reconstructs exposures that standard accounting can obscure, including unrealized losses carried at amortized cost, and combines funding-fragility indicators with machine-learning risk scoring to surface institutions under stress. In validation on the 2023 banking turmoil, the framework flags the major failures ahead of time using public data alone.

  • Full U.S. banking system, monitored quarterly from public filings
  • Hidden-loss reconstruction behind amortized-cost accounting
  • Funding-fragility and deposit-run analytics
  • Machine-learning risk scoring, validated on the 2023 bank failures
