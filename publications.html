Job Market Paper

Job Market Paper
Attention Rises, Recommendations Fall
M. R. Hoque, M. Kabir Hassan, J. A. Pérez Amuedo & Reza Huston
Accepted for presentation at the FMA 2026 Annual Meeting
Firms receiving government subsidies attract rising analyst attention while recommendations fall. The paper traces how a positive cash-flow event can worsen the tone of professional opinion, and what that reveals about the information analysts actually produce.
