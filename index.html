Mohammad Rezoanul Hoque ·

How information reaches prices, financing and trading

I work in empirical corporate finance and financial institutions: what sell-side analysts and corporate insiders do with public signals, when banks' unrecognized securities losses become priced crash risk, and how machine learning and large language models can serve as measurement tools in finance.

Before finance, I spent more than seven years in applied economic research, at the Bangladesh Institute of Development Studies, the Policy Research Institute of Bangladesh and WorldFish, and on projects for the USDA, the World Bank and the Asian Development Bank. More about me
