ECON 1204: Principles of Macroeconomics

Fall 2026 · In person, LSU New Orleans · Tuesday and Thursday, 11:00 to 12:15, KH 122

GDP, unemployment and inflation and how each is measured; long-run growth and short-run business cycles; monetary and fiscal policy; international trade and finance. Textbook: Mateer and Coppock, Principles of Macroeconomics, 5th edition. Students work with FRED, BEA and BLS data and present a group project on a policy episode.

FIN 3302: Investments

Summer 2026 · Online, LSU New Orleans · Monday and Wednesday, 6:00 to 8:45 pm

Securities markets and asset classes, risk and return, diversification, the capital asset pricing model, market efficiency, bond and equity valuation, options and futures, and performance evaluation. Textbook: Bodie, Kane and Marcus, Essentials of Investments, 12th edition. Each student runs a simulated portfolio for the term and reports on it.

Course files

  • SyllabusPDF, 3 pages
  • Investment project guidelines

The midterm covered chapters 1 to 8 (without chapter 4) and the final covered chapters 10, 13, 14, 15, 17 and 18.
